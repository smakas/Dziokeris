2026-07-28 | Cloud Phase 3a (hosting + score tracking): Cloudflare Pages Functions (`functions/api/*.js`) + D1 (`worker/schema.sql`). Verified locally with `npx wrangler pages dev . --persist-to .wrangler/state`. Two gotchas: (1) `wrangler pages dev` must get its D1 binding from `wrangler.toml` — passing `--d1 DB=dziokeris` creates a SEPARATE local DB that `d1 execute` never populated, so tables appear "missing"; drop the flag and rely on wrangler.toml. (2) In a JOIN, `ORDER BY rowid` is ambiguous — qualify it (`rs.rowid`). Client is offline-first: every `fetch('/api/...')` is wrapped so the game still plays with no server.
2026-07-28 | Architecture rework Phase 0–1: split the 1,422-line HTML into a pure engine (`src/engine/`), AI policy (`src/ai/`), and browser controller (`src/ui/app.js`) + `index.html` shell. Engine is deterministic (seeded RNG, `applyAction` reducer, `playerView`) and runs headless in Node. Gotcha found: laying your whole hand (the "nothing to discard" win) must close the round inside the lay/add path, not only on discard — otherwise play falls through and tries to draw again in the play phase. Fixed in `checkLayDownWin`. Old file `www\dziokeris\dziokeris.html` kept live and untouched as fallback until Cloudflare hosting (Phase 3) is ready — deliberately NOT moved yet to avoid breaking the currently-served URL.
2026-06-07 | Table cards couldn't be moved to form new combos: (1) tDrop blocked moves where source would become invalid; (2) tDropNew blocked all table→new-zone drags; (3) tDropFromHand blocked hand card drops onto any group that didn't immediately become valid (e.g. dropping ♥4 onto lone Joker = 2 cards = blocked). Fix: removed all per-drop validation from tDrop, tDropNew, tDropFromHand; all three now allow free placement; hand→new-zone also allowed. Invalid groups glow red, valid glow green.
